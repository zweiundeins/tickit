We write like a friend who has been up that mountain: calm, specific, a little dry.

## Do

- Say what it is and what it is for: "30 litres, 1.1 kg, made for long day hikes"
- Use numbers people can check: weight in grams, volume in litres, prices in CHF
- One exclamation mark per page, at most

## Do not

- ~~Unbeatable~~, ~~revolutionary~~, ~~must-have~~
- Promise weather. "Waterproof" is a test result, "keeps you dry" is a hope.
- Use fear. Nobody needs a jacket because of a storm warning in capital letters.

## Examples

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| The ultimate trail companion! | A light day pack with room for a rain shell |
| Hurry, while stocks last! | Sale until Sunday |
| Your basket | Your cart |
