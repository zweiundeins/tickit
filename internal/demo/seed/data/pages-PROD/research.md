Twelve interviews of 30 minutes, by video, with customers who bought hiking gear twice or more this year. Notes by @s-amara and @s-priya.

## What people do today

- 5 keep a note on the phone, 2 a spreadsheet, 2 a photo of a paper list, 3 nothing
- Nobody updates their list after a trip, everyone means to

## Quotes

> I have a spreadsheet with 140 rows. I use 30 of them. I am scared to delete the others.

> The head torch. Every time. I own four head torches because of this.

> My partner packs the food, I pack the rest, and we both pack the stove.

## What surprised us

- Weight matters more than we thought, even for day hikes
- People want to see what they already own, not only what to buy
- Nobody wanted reminders. "Don't nag me, I am an adult who forgets things."
