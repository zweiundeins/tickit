Answers we give often. Copy them, but write like a person.

## Where is my parcel?

Orders leave the warehouse within one working day. Tracking is in the email and in the app. If the app says Delivered but the parcel is not there, check the pick-up point first ([[APP-28]]), then write to us.

## Can I return sale items?

Yes. 30 days, like everything else, and the return label is free.

## Is the old price real?

Yes. The struck-through price is the lowest price of the last 30 days ([[WEB-16]]).

## The size does not fit

Send it back, we exchange it for free. Then tell us how it fit; it helps the next person ([[WEB-5]]).

## Can I delete my account?

In the app: Profile, Delete my account. On the web: write to us and we do it within a day.

## Where are you?

Online, with a warehouse in the valley. We are a small team, and we hike a lot.
