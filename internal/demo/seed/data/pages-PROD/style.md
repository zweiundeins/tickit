How Alpenglow looks and sounds, in the shop, the app and every email.

## Colours

| Name | Use | Contrast on white |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Teal 700 | buttons, links | 5.4 : 1 |
| Slate 900 | text | 17 : 1 |
| Slate 500 | secondary text | 4.8 : 1 |
| Rose 700 | sale prices only | 6.1 : 1 |
| Amber 500 | warnings, never text | |

Teal 700 replaced Teal 600 for buttons this month, for AA contrast ([[WEB-26]]).

## Type

One typeface, two weights: regular and semibold. Headings are semibold, never bold and never all capitals, except the small labels in diagrams.

## Pictures

Our own pixel pictures for banners and illustrations, real photos for products. No stock photos of people laughing at salads.

![Trail signpost](lib:trail-signpost)

## Words

Cart, not basket. Sign in, not log in. More in [[page:Writing product copy]] and [[page:Glossary]].
